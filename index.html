Inside the concept

Ingredients with purpose.

Our ingredient vision combines traditional chocolate-making ingredients with nuts, seeds, fruit and carefully selected nutritional ingredients.

◈
01 · CHOCOLATE BASE

Fine cacao

Single-origin cacao paste, cocoa butter and high-quality cocoa ingredients for a rich chocolate flavour.

✧
02 · CREAMINESS

A2 milk inspiration

Grass-fed A2 whole milk powder is a proposed ingredient for a creamy milk-chocolate profile. Suitability and allergen labelling would need verification.

❋
03 · NATURAL SWEETNESS

Date & coconut

Date nectar crystals or coconut blossom sugar are possible alternatives to refined white sugar. They still count as sugars.

◉
04 · NATURAL FLAVOUR

Vanilla & sea salt

Madagascar Bourbon vanilla bean and a little Celtic sea salt to complement the cacao flavour.

◇
05 · PRALINE

Piedmont hazelnut

Stone-ground hazelnut butter for a smooth, nutty praline character and a luxurious mouthfeel.

✦
06 · SEED BLEND

Pumpkin & hemp

Pumpkin seed butter, hemp hearts, flaxseed and chia as proposed sources of fats, fibre and plant nutrients.

❀
07 · BERRY BLEND

Wild berries

Freeze-dried bilberry and raspberry powders for fruity flavour and naturally occurring plant compounds.

◌
08 · EMULSION

Sunflower lecithin

A commonly used chocolate ingredient that helps with flow and texture during manufacturing.

✧
09 · FIBRE

Whole-food fibre

Potential fibre sources from seeds and fruit, with final quantities determined by nutritional testing.

Ingredient note: These are proposed ingredients for a product concept, not a tested recipe. Nuts, milk and seeds can cause allergies. Final ingredient choices, quantities, processing and food-safety checks must be developed and verified by qualified food professionals.
