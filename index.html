The ingredient philosophy

Nothing ordinary.
Only considered choices.

A proposed ingredient direction built around quality, flavour and a thoughtful approach to sweetness.

THE
COCOA
COLLECTION
  • 01

    Fine cocoa mass

    The foundation of the chocolate, selected for a rich and distinctive cocoa character.

  • 02

    Pure cocoa butter

    Helps create the smooth texture and elegant melt associated with fine chocolate.

  • 03

    Natural vanilla

    A carefully balanced flavour note that complements the depth of cocoa.

  • 04

    Roasted hazelnut

    An optional signature variation with a warm, nutty flavour.

  • 05

    Chicory root fibre

    An ingredient that could be explored in a carefully tested sweetness formulation.

  • 06

    A touch of sea salt

    A small, considered addition to help balance the overall flavour.

FORMULATION NOTE: This is a proposed ingredient concept, not a final recipe. Ingredient quantities, sweetening system, nutritional values, allergens and legal product claims require professional development and testing before launch.
The world of BUBLY

Make them notice.
Make them remember.

Our promotion strategy is about more than advertising. We want to build a recognisable luxury brand that people are excited to discover, share and experience.

01 / SOCIAL MEDIA

Instagram and TikTok

Create cinematic chocolate videos, close-up shots of the bubbly texture, satisfying chocolate-breaking moments, behind-the-scenes clips and product reveals. Build curiosity before the launch.

02 / CREATORS

Food and lifestyle influencers

Work with carefully selected food creators, luxury lifestyle reviewers and chocolate enthusiasts. Send exclusive tasting boxes and invite honest reviews.

03 / REAL EXPERIENCES

Luxury tasting events

Host elegant tasting experiences in selected cafés, boutique hotels, food festivals and specialist chocolate stores. Let people experience the product rather than simply seeing an advert.

04 / BRAND PARTNERSHIPS

Premium locations

Approach boutique hotels, high-end cafés, gift shops, gourmet food retailers and carefully selected department stores to introduce BUBLY to customers who enjoy premium products.

05 / PUBLIC RELATIONS

Get people talking

Send launch announcements and tasting invitations to food magazines, local newspapers, food bloggers and relevant lifestyle publications.

06 / EXCLUSIVE LAUNCH

Create anticipation

Reveal the packaging in stages, introduce the signature chocolate bar with a cinematic launch film and create a limited first tasting experience.

OUR MAIN PROMOTIONAL IDEA

The BUBLY First Taste Experience.

Invite selected creators, food reviewers and potential retail partners to an elegant chocolate tasting. Introduce the product through its story, texture, flavour and packaging. Capture authentic reactions and turn the event into a series of social media videos. The goal is to create genuine interest and word of mouth, not just another advertisement.
