03 / Ingredient philosophy

Good choices.
Great chocolate.

Our proposed recipe direction starts with quality cocoa and cocoa butter, with natural vanilla for flavour.

Other possible ingredients include roasted hazelnut, chicory root fibre and a small amount of sea salt, depending on the final recipe.

We want to explore a chocolate that balances taste, ingredient quality and a more considered approach to sweetness.

The final recipe would need proper food development and nutrition testing before making any health or sugar-free claims.
